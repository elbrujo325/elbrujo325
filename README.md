## Research

**B.Sc. thesis (UNMSM, 2026)** — *Perturbative analysis of the photon sphere and shadow detectability of a weakly charged Kerr–Newman black hole.*
Analytical perturbation theory plus numerical geodesics (RK4 in Python), compared against public Event Horizon Telescope visibility data for **Sgr A\*** and **M87\***. The fit is reported with its limits: the improvement for Sgr A\* is a scale effect inside a 1D symmetric model, not a detection of charge.

**Speaker, XXXI Peruvian Physics Symposium (Dec 2025)** — computational research talk.

**Volunteer researcher** — IEEE NPSS (MHD plasma simulations with FLASH) and GMCAM (numerical Schrödinger-equation modeling in Python and Fortran).

Related repos: [GR-Simulator](https://github.com/elbrujo325/GR-Simulator) · [FLASH-MHD-Analysis](https://github.com/elbrujo325/FLASH-MHD-Analysis)
